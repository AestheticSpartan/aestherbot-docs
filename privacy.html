Aestherbot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

1. Data We Store

Aestherbot may store limited Discord-related information required for functionality, including:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Server IDs
  • Inventory, eggs, pets, economy progress, and game data
  • Cooldowns and gameplay statistics
  • Settings required for bot functionality

2. Why We Store Data

Data is stored to enable gameplay systems, progression, inventories, moderation functionality, and user experience features.

3. Data Sharing

Aestherbot does not sell personal data. Data may only be shared when legally required or necessary for bot operation.

4. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Data Removal

Users may request removal of their stored data where applicable.

6. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated over time.

7. Contact

For questions, join the official Aestherbot Discord server.
